Are airflow or cooling concerns uneven between rooms?

For a furnace and AC cleaning project in Ilderton, identify the equipment model and the components included in the proposal. Blower assemblies and evaporator coils need different access and cleaning methods; a general furnace cleaning description may not cover both. If rooms have uneven airflow or cooling concerns, note when they occur and compare them with filter condition, thermostat settings, and visible equipment details. Ask how the blower, coil, drain pan, and condensate path will be handled, and what components must be protected during the work. Cleaning scope should follow inspection, not an assumed equipment age.

Do fans run while steam or cooking odours linger?

For an HRV or ventilation cleaning project in Ilderton, first determine which equipment the home has: an HRV with a heat-exchange core, bathroom exhaust fans, a kitchen range hood, or some combination. A fan that runs does not confirm that the connected duct is clear or that air reaches the exterior. Photograph model labels and accessible filter sizes, and note rooms where steam or cooking odours linger. Ask whether the proposed scope includes the core, screens, condensate path, exhaust ducts, and exterior caps, since each requires separate inspection.

Does an odour seem stronger near certain vents?

For a duct sanitizing or deodorizing inquiry in Ilderton, first describe the odour, when it occurs, and whether it is strongest near particular registers. Odours can have sources outside ductwork, so ask how those possibilities will be considered before a treatment is proposed. If duct treatment is appropriate, compare scopes that place it after cleaning rather than over visible debris. Ask for the product name, intended use, label directions, application method, and written details. A treatment should not be presented as a health measure or a substitute for finding the odour source.
